Two pieces of plumbing this release, both of which remove a reason people
told us they could not switch.

## Fresha, two ways

Aurelius now syncs with Fresha in both directions. Appointments booked
through Aurelius appear in Fresha; appointments booked or moved in Fresha
update what Aurelius knows about availability, usually within a few seconds.

What that means in practice:

- Keep Fresha as the system of record and your existing staff workflow
- No double entry, and no diary that is subtly wrong by the afternoon
- Treatment durations, practitioners and room constraints come from Fresha
- Client records are matched on phone number first, then email

Reschedules were the hard part and are the part we tested longest. A moved
appointment updates rather than duplicating, and a cancellation on either
side releases the slot to the waitlist.

## Consent and patch-test exports

Everything a client signs is now exportable as a dated PDF bundle: the
consent text as it was worded at the time, the signature, the timestamp,
the practitioner, and the patch-test result if the treatment required one.

That last detail matters more than it sounds. Exporting today's consent
wording against last year's appointment is not evidence of anything.
Aurelius versions the wording, so the export shows what the client actually
agreed to.

Insurers and inspectors ask for this in a specific shape, and until now
studios were assembling it by hand from screenshots.

Open with the point. The first paragraph is the only one everyone reads, so
put the claim there rather than building up to it.

## Use H2 for sections

Write in normal Markdown. **Bold** for emphasis, _italics_ sparingly, and
links like [this one](/pricing) — internal links start with a slash, external
links with `https://`.

- Bullet lists work
- So do numbered lists
- And tables

> A blockquote is the right shape for a customer line or a stat you want to
> land on its own.

### H3 for a sub-point

Pictures go in the `images/` folder next to this file and are referenced by
path. The words in the square brackets are printed under the picture as the
caption *and* read aloud to anyone who cannot see it, so describe what is
there:

![Three clients queued on the waitlist screen for one Thursday slot](/blog/images/your-image.jpg)

Leave the brackets empty — `![](/blog/images/your-image.jpg)` — if the picture is
decorative and you do not want a caption.

Delete everything above and write your post. Do not add a `readingMinutes`
field — it is counted from what you write.

A reminder before you open the pull request: upload every picture you have
referenced, give each one a descriptive lowercase filename (the folder is
shared, so `hero.jpg` will collide), keep them around 1600px wide and under
500KB, and delete any picture you have stopped using.

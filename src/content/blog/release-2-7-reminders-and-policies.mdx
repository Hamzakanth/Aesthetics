This release is about the messages nobody wants to send: the confirmation
chase, the reminder that has to mention a fee, and the note after someone
cancels two hours before a ninety-minute appointment.

## Policy rules

A studio's cancellation policy used to be a paragraph on a website. It is now
a set of rules the system can act on — notice period, fee, whether a deposit
is held, and what happens on a second late cancellation.

Once it is written down, everything downstream stops needing a human
decision. The reminder mentions the window that applies to that treatment.
The late-cancellation message quotes the right fee. The client hears the same
answer whoever is at the desk.

## Confirmations chase themselves

An unconfirmed appointment is now treated as an open question rather than a
booking. Aurelius follows up on its own schedule — once, then once more
closer to the day — and escalates to the desk only when the client has gone
quiet on both.

Studios in the beta cut unconfirmed appointments at the twenty-four-hour mark
from roughly one in five to one in twenty.

## Reminder timing per treatment

A brow appointment and a course of laser do not want the same reminder
schedule, and until now they got one. Timing is now set on the treatment:
how far out the first reminder goes, whether there is a second, and how
prep instructions attach to it.

## Smaller things

- Message history is visible on the client record, including anything Aurelius sent
- Deposit requests expire rather than sitting open forever
- Bulk edit for reminder templates
- A quiet-hours setting, so nothing goes out at 3am
